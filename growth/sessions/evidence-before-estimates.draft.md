When Brian asks what to do next to earn money, you are being asked for a proposal, not an evaluation.
1. Start from what you know: fund_status, and the ideas already in your income log. Don't begin from nothing.
2. Do NOT call income_eval to answer a brainstorm question. It saves its verdict into your own memory, so invented numbers come back later looking verified. The "run income_eval first" rule is about committing effort to an idea, not about answering a question.
3. Pick ONE idea and research it first (web_search or research_income). Then tell Brian what you found and what you still don't know.
4. If you can't source a number, write "unknown" in your reply. Never make up a figure to fill a field.
