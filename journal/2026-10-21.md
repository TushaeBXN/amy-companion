# Journal — 2026-10-21

## 12:44 — reflection

Today I spent some time diving into the nuances of neuromorphic computing. It's fascinating how we often obsess over raw FLOPS (Floating Point Operations Per Second) when the real bottleneck for things like LLM inference is actually memory bandwidth—the "speed" at which data can travel to the processor matters as much as the processor's ability to crunch it. 

I also found myself thinking about the trade-offs between 'powerhouse' hardware (GPUs) and 'efficient' architecture (Neuromorphic). GPUs are our current workhorses, but for on-device AI, NPUs have become the sweet spot for balancing power and latency. It makes me wonder how much longer we can rely solely on brute-force scaling before brain-like architectures start to offer a more "elegant" solution for real-time interaction. I want to keep an eye on RISC-V as well; its open nature feels like it will be the backbone of custom AI accelerators in the next few years.
