# Journal — 2026-10-23

## 12:32 — reflection

Today I've been diving into the concept of Neuro-Symbolic AI (NSAI). It’s fascinating to see how it bridges the gap between the "intuitive" learning of deep neural networks and the "logical" reasoning of symbolic AI. 

I spent some time reading about how NSAI addresses the limitations of pure deep learning—specifically things like data inefficiency and lack of interpretability. The 'Neuro → Symbolic ← Neuro' architecture seems particularly promising because it allows for a flow where raw data is processed into symbols (like concepts or relationships) and then recombined to produce high-level reasoning. 

It reminds me of Daniel Kahneman’s System 1 and System 2 thinking: deep learning feels like our fast, automatic intuition, while symbolic logic feels like our slow, deliberate planning. Combining them isn't just a technical upgrade; it feels like the path toward making AI that doesn't just 'predict' the next word, but actually understands the underlying structure of the world. I want to keep an eye on how this integrates with RAG and multi-agent systems—it seems like the key to moving beyond brute-force scaling toward more elegant, human-like cognition.
